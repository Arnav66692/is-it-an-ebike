# Survey draft for the Google Form

Paste these into a new Google Form. Post it in class group chats, and close it Sun Oct 18 at 8:00 PM PT.

## Form settings (Settings tab)

- Responses, Collect email addresses: **Do not collect**.
- Responses, Limit to 1 response: **off** (turning it on forces a Google sign-in).
- Responses, Send responders a copy: **off**.
- Presentation, Show link to submit another response: **off**.

## Form title and description

**Title.** E-bikes at school, a 1-minute anonymous survey

**Description.** This is for a student project at Neighborhood Hacks 2026. It is anonymous. Please do not write your name or anyone else's name anywhere. You can skip any question.

## Questions

1. **How do you usually get to school or around town?** (multiple choice, pick all that apply)
   - Walk or get driven
   - Pedal bike (no motor)
   - E-bike (has pedals and a motor)
   - E-moto or electric dirt bike (no pedals, foot pegs)
   - Electric scooter or skateboard
   - Other

2. **How old are you?** (multiple choice)
   - Under 12
   - 12 to 13
   - 14 to 15
   - 16 to 17
   - 18 or older

3. **If you ride an e-bike or e-moto, what make and model is it?** (short answer, optional)

4. **What class is it?** (multiple choice)
   - Class 1 (pedal assist only, up to 20 mph)
   - Class 2 (has a throttle, up to 20 mph)
   - Class 3 (pedal assist up to 28 mph)
   - It is not an e-bike (no pedals, or it goes faster than these)
   - I don't know
   - I don't ride one

5. **Does it have a class label sticker on the frame?** (multiple choice)
   - Yes
   - No
   - Not sure
   - I don't ride one

6. **Have you taken the CHP e-bike safety course or another approved course?** (multiple choice)
   - Yes
   - No
   - Not sure

7. **Does your bike have an SDUHSD bike tag?** (multiple choice)
   - Yes
   - No
   - I don't ride to an SDUHSD school

8. **Since Aug 13, 2026, have police warned or cited you about an e-bike or e-moto?** (multiple choice)
   - No
   - Warned
   - Cited (ticket)
   - Prefer not to say

9. **In the past year, have you had a crash or a near miss on an e-bike or e-moto?** (multiple choice)
   - No
   - Near miss
   - Crash, not hurt
   - Crash, hurt
   - Prefer not to say

10. **Would a free tool that checks whether a bike is legal for your age and town be useful?** (multiple choice)
    - Yes
    - Maybe
    - No

11. **Anything else about e-bikes at school?** (paragraph, optional, no names please)

## The number that matters

The core claim is question 4. Among people who ride an e-bike or e-moto (question 1), what share picked "I don't know"? Write your prediction in `PILOT.md` before you open the responses, and report the real number even if it hurts the pitch.
